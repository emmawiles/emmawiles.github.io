I take pleasure in working with and talking to students at all stages of their education. I am always on the look out for economics minded PhD students interested in HR, labor, and how AI is impacting how people find and do work. I have also enjoyed working with ambitious masters level students through the HIRE Lab. I welcome your emails, but I want to be clear about one thing: I receive a lot of page long emails with almost identical text which tell me very little about the person reaching out. These emails are completely indistinguishable from each other, and I do not always respond to them as I feel I have never learned anything about the student reaching out. 

When you reach out to me, please use your own words. It can be short! A bullet point or two is fine! Use AI to check for typos, but please do not send me an email generated off of my own website. I would much rather hear, briefly and imperfectly, from you.
